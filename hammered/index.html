Hammered Sell

Every property can sell. The only question is how fast you need it gone.

Speed, certainty and price pull against each other. Nobody explains that to sellers. The agent doesn't mention auction, the auction house doesn't mention private treaty, and nobody offers a cash exit alongside either. We put all three on the table and price them.

Exit One

Cash

Completed in 14 days

We take it to our cash buyer network and come back with a real figure you can accept or refuse. Contracts exchanged, money in, done inside a fortnight of the first phone call.

Exit Three

Open market

Three to six months

If your property is straightforward, mortgageable and you have the time, the open market usually wins on price. We don't do private treaty, so we'll introduce you to agents we rate, and they'll know the introduction came from us.

What we do once you've chosen

  • The number. Two decades of auction results behind one figure. The one that gets a completion, not the one that wins the instruction.
  • Legal pack in 48 hours. Most sellers wait three to six weeks. That's a month off your sale before you've done anything.
  • The right room. We're independent, so your lot goes to the auction house that suits it, plus our own community of active buyers.
  • Run to completion. Marketing, viewings, enquiries, solicitors, auction day. One point of contact. You get updates, not a job.

What it costs

We don't make money unless your property sells. That doesn't change on any route.

The only question on the auction route is who funds the upfront costs. Take our recommended reserve and we cover them. We set the number, so we carry the cost of being wrong about it.

Set your own reserve above ours and you fund them: £1,000 + VAT, at cost. There's no profit in that figure. We'll still take the instruction and work it just as hard. We just can't underwrite a number we didn't set.

Why us

We're independent. That's the whole thing.

We'll tell you not to use us

An auction house takes every lot it can. An agent wins the instruction and works out the price later. We'll tell you when the open market is the better route and point you at someone good, because the wrong route costs you more than our fee is ever worth.

We're in the market ourselves

We buy, we sell, and we trade. Everything we tell you comes from transactions we've done, not a course we once attended. When we say a number won't get a completion, it's because we've watched it fail.

One conversation, every route

Cash, auction, open market. Buying, selling, or working out which. You don't have to understand how our business is structured before you can get a straight answer out of us.

Our buyers are why sellers win

The community we've built on the buying side is what puts a room of funded, experienced bidders in front of every lot we sell. No auction house can offer a seller that, because none of them have spent six years building one.
