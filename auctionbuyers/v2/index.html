The room

Twenty-four people, and we interview every one of them.

Members come from banking, medicine, professional sport, the trades and full-time property. Some have been buying at auction for twenty years. Some bought their first lot after joining.

What they have in common is that they are all actually transacting. Nobody is watching from the sidelines, nobody is here to sell you anything, and nobody guards their deals. Members openly discuss properties they're interested in, because the room is worth more to them than any single lot.

Trust is the currency here. It only works because we're careful about who comes in, and because the moment the room gets bigger than twenty-four, your lot stops getting looked at properly. That is the entire product, so the cap doesn't move.

Who it's for

We say no to people. That's what keeps it worth joining.

Twenty-four seats. Every one of them held by someone who can act on what the room tells them. Here's the honest bar:

£100,000+ ready to deploy

Cash for your own deals, or an unencumbered or low-geared portfolio. Not because we're precious — because the learning only lands when you can act on it.

Buying now, not “one day”

Not next year, and not once you've read a bit more. The room moves at the pace of real auctions.

You do your homework

Go through the catalogues. Turn up. Research your own lots. We'll give you an honest read on any property — we won't do it for you.

You put your experience in

Everyone in the room benefits from everyone else's scars. Yours count too.

Not quite there yet? Apply anyway and say so. We'll tell you honestly and point you at what to do first. Timing matters more than ambition in this game — and there's no shame in being early.
