The members, in their own words

You'll get a better sense of this from the people in the room than from anything we write.

Tuesday and Thursday, 8:45am

We review over 1,000 auction lots a year. Most buyers manage five.

Ten to fifteen lots a call. Two calls a week. Every week. Not a webinar and not a lecture. A room of active buyers going through real properties that real people are about to bid on.

You bring a lot

  • Something you found in this cycle's catalogue
  • A deal you can't make your mind up about
  • One you're already committed to and want checked
  • Or nothing at all, and you still get fourteen others

We tell you straight

  • Whether it's a good deal, or whether you walk away
  • What it's actually worth, not what the guide suggests
  • What it's going to need, and what that will cost
  • What to bid, and how to structure the offer

Then you listen to us do the same for everyone else's. Fourteen more properties you would never have looked at, priced and pulled apart in front of you, by people with capital in the market this month.

A buyer working alone properly assesses maybe five to ten lots a year. A member sits through a thousand, and when one of them buys, they come back and tell the room what actually happened.

You're not here to learn how auctions work. You're here to borrow other people's experience at a rate that isn't available on your own.

The reason an experienced trader spots the unmortgageable flat, the short lease or the £40,000 of works in ninety seconds isn't that they're cleverer than you. It's that they've seen it go wrong before.

We give you the reps without the losses.

The room

Twenty-four people, and we interview every one of them.

Members come from banking, medicine, professional sport, the trades and full-time property. Some have been buying at auction for twenty years. Some bought their first lot after joining.

What they have in common is that they are all actually transacting. Nobody is watching from the sidelines, nobody is here to sell you anything, and nobody guards their deals. Members openly discuss properties they're interested in, because the room is worth more to them than any single lot.

Trust is the currency here. It only works because we're careful about who comes in, and because the moment the room gets bigger than twenty-four, your lot stops getting looked at properly. That is the entire product, so the cap doesn't move.

Who it's for

We say no to people. That's what keeps it worth joining.

£100,000+ liquid

Cash available for your own deals, or an unencumbered or low-geared portfolio. Not because we're precious, but because the learning only lands when you can act on it. Watching teaches you nothing.

Ready to transact

Now. Not next year, and not once you've read a bit more.

Willing to do the work

Go through the catalogues before the call. Turn up for the calls in your auction cycle. Research your own lots. We'll give you an honest read on any property. We won't do your homework.

Willing to share

Everyone in the room is getting the benefit of other people's experience. You're expected to put yours in.

If that isn't you yet, apply anyway and say so. We'll tell you honestly and point you at what to do first. Timing matters more than ambition in this game, and there's no shame in being early.
